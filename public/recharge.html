📐 Transparent Value Methodology & Source Integrity

All tariffs displayed on this dashboard reflect official post-July 2024 revision pricing and TRAI mandatory tariff filings. We do not invent plan rates, extrapolate unsupported perks, or fabricate 5G access.

Cost per Day: Price (₹) ÷ Validity (Days) — the true recurring daily expenditure.
Cost per GB: Price (₹) ÷ Total Guaranteed GB — calculated only when data quota is finite and known.
Value Score (0–100): Weighted composite measuring daily allowance (35%), pack validity (25%), daily expenditure frugality (25%), genuine 5G eligibility (10%), and verified OTT bonus perks (5%).
